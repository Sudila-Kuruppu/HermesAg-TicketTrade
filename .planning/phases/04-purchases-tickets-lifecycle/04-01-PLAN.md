<objective>
Land the Phase 4 substrate: the four Phase 4 migrations (`tickets`, `users.redeemed_count`, `reports`, `audit_log` stub), the `Support\\Audit` stub (forward-compatible with Phase 8 hash chain), the `Ticket\\Model\\ticket_model` + `Ticket\\Service\\ticket_service` pair (sole writer of `tickets` per AD-1; honors every AD-9 atomic UPDATE guard), the four new ticket-context Actions (`BuyAction`, `RedeemAction`, `ConfirmSessionAction`, `DisputeAction`), `Points\\Service\\points_service::awardTransaction()` (Phase 4 stub honoring FR-PTS-007 + FR-PTS-010 only), the `Support\\RateLimit::hit()` 3rd-param extension (so `redemption:ticket:{id}:{user}` scopes correctly per D-08), the three new View partials (`ticket_code_block`, `session_progress`, `status_badge`), and the `ticket-code-block` JS component (mask/reveal/copy/WhatsApp). Purpose: this plan is the Wave-1 foundation that plans 02 (real ticket-context pages), 03 (cron extension), and the Phase 5/6/7 follow-ups all depend on.
